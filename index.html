Small studio,sharp output

KROMA is a two-person studio that takes on a handful of projects a year and gives each one its own identity. No templates, no recycled layouts, no beige. We care about the parts most people skip: how a page loads, how a button feels, how color moves when you scroll.

What we do

  • Logos, palettes, type systems and the rules that keep them alive. Built to survive contact with the real world.

  • Screens that carry a point of view. Layout, motion and color treated as one system, not three meetings.

  • Hand-built front ends with buttery scroll and 60fps motion. Fast on throttled phones, not just on our machines.

  • Updates, tweaks and seasonal refreshes after launch. Sites are gardens, not sculptures.
